Kiri Cars Community Guidelines

Kiri Stories welcomes automotive and non-automotive writing, learning, creativity, personal experiences, opinions, fiction and entertainment. We want a useful, respectful and safe community.

Content we welcome

  • Automotive knowledge, repairs, reviews, ownership experiences and industry discussion.
  • Educational and informative articles, tutorials and thoughtful opinions.
  • Personal stories, creative writing, fiction, humour and entertainment.
  • Images you created or are authorised to use, with captions where helpful.

Content and behaviour not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, targeted abuse or encouragement of violence.
  • Hate or dehumanising attacks against people based on protected characteristics.
  • Scams, fraud, impersonation, deceptive advertising or deliberate misinformation presented as fact.
  • Defamation, doxxing, private personal information shared without lawful authority, or intimate imagery shared without consent.
  • Copyright- or trademark-infringing text, photographs, illustrations or other material.
  • Sexual exploitation, child sexual abuse material, or content that exploits or endangers minors.
  • Instructions or promotion for serious wrongdoing, malware, or dangerous activities likely to cause harm.
  • Spam, repetitive promotional posts, malicious links or attempts to manipulate likes, comments or ratings.

Author responsibility

The person who submits an article is responsible for its text, images, claims and permissions. Identify fiction and opinion clearly where readers could mistake them for factual reporting. Do not present unverified allegations about identifiable people or businesses as established facts.

Images and captions

Only upload images you own or are permitted to use. Do not publish images that expose private information, mislead readers about a vehicle or event, or violate another person's privacy or rights.

Reporting and enforcement

Use the report feature to flag content or accounts and provide a clear reason. Do not abuse reporting to silence disagreement. Kiri Cars reviews reports and may take action proportionate to the issue, including asking for edits, limiting visibility, removing content, issuing warnings, or suspending or banning accounts. Where available, users may appeal a decision.

Respectful disagreement

Debate ideas, not people's dignity. Criticism and differing opinions are welcome when expressed without harassment, threats or knowingly false allegations.
